# Onboarding layout: the button stays on short screens

_`OnboardingLayout` is `h-dvh` with its own vertical scroll and a sticky button bar. Until 2026-10-19 it was `h-screen overflow-hidden`, so on any screen shorter than the content the Next button was clipped away with no way to scroll to it._

Found 2026-10-19 after an invitee on Firefox 158 / Android 16 (Galaxy A35, 384×832 CSS px) answered survey questions 1 to 4 on the web, then resubmitted question 4 three times, reloaded three times and installed the app instead (Supabase `edge_logs`, 2026-10-18 ≈ 14:22–14:25 UTC). Question 4 leads to `/values/4`.

The three video value screens are taller than many visible viewports because `PhoneMockup` renders the video at a fixed `h-[28rem]` (464 px with its frame). Measured content height in English: `/values/2` 712 px, `/values/3` 720 px, `/values/4` 712 px at 384 px wide and 752 px at 360 px wide; `/values/1` (no video) fits at 640. In a 360×640 viewport the old layout put Next at 640–728 px, fully below the fold and unreachable on `/values/2`, `/3` and `/4`; at 384×690 it touched or crossed the edge. `100vh` in a mobile browser is the viewport with the bars hidden, so the visible part is shorter still while the bars show. Small Android phones were affected inside the app too, wherever the WebView is shorter than the content.

The fix (one file): `h-dvh` sizes the layout to the visible viewport, `overflow-y-auto` lets content scroll inside it (`overflow-x-hidden` keeps clipping the import screen's floating icons as before), and the Next bar is `sticky bottom-0 bg-background`. The bar carries the bottom padding (`pb-6`, the container drops to `pt-6` when there is a button) because a sticky element stops at the scroll container's padding edge, and the 24 px below the button would otherwise show the scrolled content. When the content fits, every element sits on the same pixel as before (checked at 384×832 and 360×640 on eleven of the thirteen screens using the layout; `InviteMembers` and the native `Subscribe` were not opened). `SocialProof`, which scrolls its own testimonial list with `flex-1 min-h-0`, keeps working because the layout still has a fixed height.

Regression test: `e2e/onboarding.spec.ts` › "Value screens on a short screen" (360×640, button fully in the viewport; it fails on the old layout for `/values/2–4`).

To look at these screens with the dev login, the account must have `has_completed_survey = false`, because `guardOnboardingRoute` sends finished accounts to `/home`. The dev account can flip its own flag with a `PATCH /rest/v1/users?id=eq.<id>` under its own JWT (`Prefer: return=minimal`; asking for the row back fails with 42501, the table is not selectable). Set it back to true afterwards. The browser pane pauses animation frames while hidden, so motion components stay at their `initial` transform: measure layout with `offsetTop` / `offsetHeight`, not `getBoundingClientRect`.
