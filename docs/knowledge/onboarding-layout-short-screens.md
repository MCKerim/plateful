# Onboarding value screens fit short screens

_The video value screens (`/values/2`, `/3`, `/4`) lay out like iOS: title on top, a phone that takes whatever height is left, text and Next below. Nothing scrolls. The phone is a neutral CSS frame whose screen keeps the recordings' 1080×2424 ratio. Since 2026-10-19._

**Layout.** Each screen wraps its hero in `.phone-stage` (`src/components/onboarding/phoneMockup/PhoneMockup.css`): `flex: 1 1 0; min-height: 0; container-type: size`. `PhoneMockup` sizes itself from that container with `height: min(100cqh, (100cqw - var(--phone-gutter)) / 0.4662)` and `aspect-ratio: 0.4662`. The import screen sets `--phone-gutter: 8rem` on `.phone-with-icons` so its floating icons stay on screen. Measured in English: 160×344 px at 360×640 (145×312 on `/values/4`, whose title has a subtitle), 224 to 254 px wide at 384×832, and everything fits in iPhone 16e Safari with its bars showing. WebViews without container query units (before Chrome 105) fall back to a fixed 24 rem phone.

**The frame.** `#1c1c1e` body, bezel 4 % of the phone's width on all sides, a punch-hole dot, a faint light rim so it stays visible in dark mode. The screen's insets and radii are percentages of the phone, worked out so the screen is exactly 1080:2424 and nothing of a recording is cut off; the phone's ratio follows as `1 / (0.92 × 2424 / 1080 + 0.08) = 0.4662`. All three recordings have that ratio (`planner-recording.mp4` 1080×2424, the other two 720×1616). A recording with another ratio needs new numbers in the CSS. iOS uses Apple's iPhone 17 Pro Max product art as its bezel (`ImportHero.swift`); that stays out of the Android and web app.

**`OnboardingLayout`** is `h-dvh` with `overflow-x-hidden overflow-y-auto`. The scroll is only a silent fallback for extreme cases on the other screens (landscape, very large text) and never shows when the content fits. The eight other screens using the layout (`/values/1`, `/choosename`, `/createhousehold`, `/howitworks`, `/socialproof`, `/trial`, `/trialreminder`, web `/subscribe`) keep every element on the same pixel as with the original `h-screen overflow-hidden`, checked at 384×832 and 360×640.

**History.** Until 2026-10-19 the layout was `h-screen overflow-hidden` and the video was a fixed `h-[28rem]`, so the three screens were 712 to 752 px tall and Next sat below the fold, unreachable, at 360×640 or in a phone browser with its bars showing. That is most likely where an invitee on Firefox for Android stopped on 2026-10-18 after survey question 4 (Supabase `edge_logs`: question 4 resubmitted three times, three reloads, then the app installed). Commit `be4668f` first made the layout scroll with a sticky Next bar. Kerim rejected that the same night: it looked like a quick fix, and the page title scrolled out of view. The old mockup never cropped the recording, by the way: in shrink-to-fit layout the video box took its width from the recording's own ratio.

**Tests.** `e2e/onboarding.spec.ts` › "Value screens on a short screen" at 360×640. Title, text and button must all be fully in the viewport without scrolling (fails on the original layout for `/values/2–4` and on `be4668f` for `/3` and `/4`), and the video box must have the recordings' ratio (guards the frame's numbers).

**Looking at these screens.** The dev login needs `has_completed_survey = false`, because `guardOnboardingRoute` sends finished accounts to `/home`. The dev account can flip its own flag with `PATCH /rest/v1/users?id=eq.<id>` under its own JWT (`Prefer: return=minimal`; asking for the row back fails with 42501). Set it back afterwards. The browser pane pauses animation frames while hidden, so motion components keep their `initial` transform and opacity: measure with `offsetTop` / `offsetHeight`, summed up the `offsetParent` chain to the layout, because a running transform changes which element is the `offsetParent`.
